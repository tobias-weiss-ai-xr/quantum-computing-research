Field Guide – What Is This About?
  • Quantum Computing uses quantum bits (qubits) that can be 0, 1, or both at once, enabling parallelism that classical bits cannot match. Think factoring large numbers (Shor) or simulating molecules (VQE).
  • Quantum Error Correction is the art of protecting fragile qubits against noise and errors by spreading information across many physical qubits (surface codes, LDPC codes, stabilizer circuits).
  • Quantum Algorithms & Complexity answers "How much faster can a quantum computer solve problem X?" Studies BQP vs NP, Grover search, quantum walks, and more.
  • Quantum Hardware builds the machines — superconducting circuit, trapped ions, photonic qubits, neutral atoms, silicon spin qubits. Each platform trades off coherence time, gate fidelity, scalability.
  • Quantum Machine Learning mixes quantum and classical techniques: quantum kernels, VQEs, variational circuits, quantum neural networks. Often hybrid for near-term, noisy hardware.
  • Quantum Cryptography transmits unhackable secrets via laws of physics — QKD (quantum key distribution), quantum repeaters, device-independent crypto, post-quantum security against quantum computers.
  • Quantum Information Theory is the mathematics: entanglement, entropy, channels, tomography, causality, resource theories.
  • Quantum Simulation assigns a quantum computer to simulate another quantum system (Hamiltonians, many-body dynamics) far beyond what any classical supercomputer can handle.
  • Quantum Software / Tools makes the stack usable: compilers (Qiskit, Cirq, PennyLane), transpilers, verification, fuzzing, optimisation.
  • Surveys & Taxonomies map the landscape, point you to further reading.
